Owner tools

Practical, rule-based planning from records saved on this device. All actions are drafts for you to review.

Cash survival calendar

A planning estimate, not a bank balance or cash-flow forecast. Enter the cash currently available to improve the estimate.

Smart reorder coach

Flags products at or below their low-stock threshold. Suggested order quantity is only a simple target-stock estimate.

Profit leak radar

Checks recorded expenses, missing product costs and unusually low/negative estimated margin. This is not full accounting profit.

Customer comeback radar

Finds customers with invoices but no recent invoice in the last 30 days. Message drafts require your review.

One-tap day closing

Creates a dated snapshot of today's recorded sales, expenses, invoice counts and outstanding amount. It does not lock records or reconcile a bank account.

Duplicate & mistake guard

Before saving an invoice, checks for a likely duplicate using customer, date and total. It warns but lets you proceed after confirmation. Existing stock-out checks remain active.

Privacy & limits: calculations run in this browser using locally stored app data. No paid AI API, automatic purchasing, customer messaging, or payment action is used. Keep backups; clearing browser data may erase records.
